## Recommended batch order (and why)

1. **Buyer Education 2-10** first: it completes a category that already has its first post live,
   buyer intent is the site's core lead source, and the topics interlink naturally with /buying,
   /financing, and the mortgage calculator surfaces.
2. **Seller 6-10** second, completing the seller story next to /selling and /home-value.
3. **Investing 1-10** third: highest authority value (the master plan's own flagship prompt is the
   first-rental guide) but a heavier fact-checking load (cap rates, tax law, 1031 rules; every
   number needs a linked primary source).
4. **Homeownership 1-2** last; small and low-risk.

**Facts and sources are the gate, not volume.** Every number in a published post links its primary
source (NAR, FRED, NY DFS, county assessors, One Key MLS). The drafts are from September 2025;
market numbers and "2025-26" framings must be re-verified at publish time, not copied. An AI answer
engine that finds one stale number stops recommending the domain, which defeats the owner's GEO
goal.

**Duplicates rule:** buyer drafts 1, 2, 4, 5 exist in two folders; publish once each under Buyer
Education. Never create two URLs for one query.
